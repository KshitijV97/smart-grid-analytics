# ELG 5255 Course Project: Requirements Summary

Text summary of [`Project_Overview.pdf`](Project_Overview.pdf) (ELG 5255 Applied Machine Learning, University of Ottawa, Fall 2026). If this file and the PDF disagree, the PDF wins.

## Goal
Apply ML concepts and algorithms to a real-world Electrical and Computer Engineering problem. This repository's topic area is **Smart grid**.

The project has two reporting stages:
1. A **state-of-the-art review** of applied ML algorithms in the selected topic.
2. A **code implementation** of at least one applied ML algorithm in that topic.

## Groups
- 2 to 4 students. Each member is graded on their own contribution.
- Every deliverable must clearly state each member's contribution, and all members must sign to agree.

## Timeline and deliverables
| Deadline (tentative) | Phase | Weight | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 13 | Phase 1: Project proposal | 8% | 5-min presentation: state-of-the-art review of applied ML in the topic, plus proposed ML solutions |
| Dec 8 | Phase 2: Final presentation | 10% | 10-min presentation: implementation workflow, outcomes, final remarks |
| Dec 14 | Phase 3: Final report | 12% | IEEE conference format (double-column), at least 5 pages. Must include the Phase 1 review. Sections: abstract, introduction, literature review, overview of the selected ML algorithm, implementation results, conclusion, references |

## Smart grid topic scope (from the brief)
- Load forecasting for data centers
- Renewable energy generation forecasting
- Load forecasting for residential areas
- Anomaly detection in smart grid

Sample papers listed for this topic:
- M. Mughees, Y. Li, Y. Chen and Y. R. Li, "Short-Term Load Forecasting for AI-Data Center," IEEE PESGM 2025, doi: 10.1109/PESGM52009.2025.11225317.
- Y. Zhang, J. Wang and B. Chen, "Detecting False Data Injection Attacks in Smart Grids: A Semi-Supervised Deep Learning Approach," IEEE Trans. Smart Grid, vol. 12, no. 1, pp. 623-634, Jan. 2021, doi: 10.1109/TSG.2020.3010510.

## Implementation rules
- If implementing from a paper, the paper must be high quality and **ACM/IEEE indexed**.
- Implementations without a reference paper are judged by the instructor/TA on technical contribution and feasibility.

## Evaluation criteria
- **Clarity and completeness:** clear, well-structured reports and presentations that address every part of the project.
- **Technical merit:** depth of the methods and implementation; shows real understanding of the topic.
- **Results and analysis:** results presented well and analyzed thoroughly; model performance evaluated critically.
- **Project report:** organized, well-written, free of typos, and consistent with both presentations.
